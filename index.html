01 — About us

Incepted in the year 2000, M/S Om Jyoti Engineering Enterprises is a distinguished Manufacturer, Distributor, Retailer and Service Provider of water and industrial engineering solutions.

We specialize in the design, supply, installation and maintenance of advanced water treatment systems, centrifugal pumps, industrial machinery and air compressors for residential, commercial and industrial applications.

As a trusted Fire Fighting System Contractor and Industrial Pump Supplier, we deliver complete turnkey solutions: fire hydrant and sprinkler systems, high-pressure fire pumps and pump automation panels, in compliance with industry standards. Our expertise extends to RO water treatment, packaged drinking water plants, wastewater management and water recycling.

We are recognized as a reliable STP, RO Plant and Industrial Equipment Supplier in India, with a strong focus on quality, innovation and timely project execution.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    What does Om Jyoti Engineering Enterprises do?

    Incepted in 2000, Om Jyoti Engineering Enterprises is a manufacturer, distributor, retailer and service provider of mineral water plants, industrial pumps, RO plants, fire fighting systems and rain water harvesting solutions. We design, supply, install and maintain them for residential, commercial and industrial clients.

    Where are you located, and which areas do you serve?

    Our office is at D 239, Sector 10, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301. We supply and service projects across Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, Okhla (Delhi), Agra, Kanpur, Muzaffarnagar and beyond, including South Tripura.

    Which pump brands do you supply and service?

    We deal in pumps and motors from KSB, Kirloskar, GL, Grundfos and Wilo, with authorised service support for KSB pumps. Our team handles installation, commissioning, repair and annual maintenance contracts (AMC).

    What capacities of industrial RO plants do you offer?

    Our industrial RO plants are available from 100 L to 50,000 L and use membrane technology that removes up to 99% of total dissolved solids (TDS), bacteria and harmful substances.

    Can you set up a complete mineral water plant?

    Yes. A mineral water plant setup includes filtration, an RO system, UV and ozonation, mineral dosing and automatic filling and packaging, with PET blowing, rinsing-filling-capping (RFC) and shrink wrap machines. Plants are built for BIS and FSSAI compliance.

    Which standards do your fire fighting systems follow?

    OM JYOTI EDJ fire fighting systems combine electric, diesel and jockey pumps on a compact skid, are factory tested, and are designed as per NFPA 20 / IS 12845. We also install fire hydrant and sprinkler systems.

    What sizes of sewage treatment plants (STP) do you supply?

    We offer prefabricated, modular MBBR and SBR sewage treatment plants from 1 KLD to 500 KLD and higher, needing minimal civil work, with treated water suitable for reuse.

    Do you install rain water harvesting systems?

    Yes. Our modular rain water harvesting systems collect, filter, store and recharge rainwater, with high-strength underground storage modules that can be installed under roads and parking areas.
